## Closing

> From cyclic to linear, from absolute to relative, from the objective clock to subjective duration, from the thermodynamic arrow to “time is not fundamental” in quantum gravity — the inquiry into the nature of time has still reached no verdict. Time series analysis, beneath this far grander inquiry, humbly deals with “data in time”.
